The Artist Behind the Lens

Hello, I’m Elena Vance. I frame fleeting moments into heirloom art.

Based between New York and the Pacific Northwest, my work is rooted in quiet observation, honest human connection, and sculptural natural light. Over the past decade, I have traveled across four continents documenting intimate love stories, visionary creatives, and untamed landscapes.

My Philosophy: Great photography doesn’t feel staged—it feels remembered. By blending editorial direction with unscripted documentary stillness, I create photographs that remain evocative fifty years from today.
